## 1. The Banned AI Word & Phrase Registry

| AI Telltale Cliche | Authentic Academic Replacement |
| :--- | :--- |
| "delve into" | "investigate", "examine", "analyze", "explore" |
| "plays a pivotal / crucial role" | "is essential for", "governs", "drives", "moderates" |
| "a testament to" | "demonstrates", "indicates", "corroborates" |
| "rich tapestry / vibrant landscape" | "complex domain", "heterogeneous setting", "framework" |
| "In summary / In conclusion" | *Direct thesis synthesis without explicit transition flags* |
| "It is important to remember that" | "Notably,", "Critically,", or omit introductory filler |
| "fosters collaboration" | "enables coordination", "facilitates team integration" |

## 2. Burstiness & Perplexity Calibration
Human scholars vary sentence rhythm instinctively:
- **Robotic AI Pattern (Monotonous length ~18 words):**  
  *"The algorithm processes genomic datasets efficiently. It calculates the alignment scores rapidly. Furthermore, the results demonstrate high statistical significance."*
- **Humanized Academic Prose (High variance 6 to 32 words):**  
  *"Alignment speed matters. By offloading sequence mapping to vectorized SIMD instructions, the pipeline achieves an 8.4-fold acceleration over standard baseline parsers—a gain that proves critical when processing multi-gigabyte FASTQ archives under tight memory constraints."*

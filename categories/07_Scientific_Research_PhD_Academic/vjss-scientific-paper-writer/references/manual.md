## 1. The 5-Sentence High-Impact Abstract Architecture
1. **Sentence 1 (Broad Context):** Establish why the domain is critical to science or engineering.
2. **Sentence 2 (Knowledge Gap / Bottleneck):** State the fundamental unsolved problem in existing state-of-the-art literature.
3. **Sentence 3 (Our Contribution):** Introduce your methodology/system/framework concisely.
4. **Sentence 4 (Key Quantitative Finding):** State the most impressive statistical metric (e.g., "Achieving a 34.2% reduction in latency and 99.4% accuracy across benchmark datasets").
5. **Sentence 5 (Broader Significance):** Explain how this result unlocks new research paradigms or industrial applications.
